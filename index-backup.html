About me

I’m a WordPress developer with over a year of hands-on experience building and maintaining production websites, from custom themes to live hosting, deployments and client revisions.

I work mostly in PHP, Custom Post Types, ACF and WooCommerce hooks, building flexible, maintainable content structures rather than relying only on page builders. A past Python full stack internship (Django and Angular) is why I reach for REST APIs and clean MVC structure, even inside a CMS.
